# Working first offer

Status: engineering scope assumption, October 2, 2026. Not a validated buyer offer
or a statement that all capabilities below are implemented.

Sellora is a managed service for a small company's staff to draft repeated buyer
replies from company-approved documents. The user intends paid setup and ongoing
maintenance. Small B2B software/IT-service teams are a buyer hypothesis; no buyer
or industry has been selected. Proposed pricing of $1,000 setup and $500–660/month
remains provisional until value, costs and support effort are measured.

## One task

1. A company document owner approves current source material and its permitted
   audience. An admin maintains that organization's knowledge library.
2. A staff member pastes a buyer question. Initial inputs are a question and the
   approved document scope; automatic email intake comes after manual acceptance.
3. Sellora presents a draft with inspectable sources, or an explicit refusal when
   evidence is insufficient. Source ranking is not a probability of correctness.
4. An authorized company reviewer inspects sources, corrects the draft and approves
   that specific revision. Reviewer identity/permissions must be agreed before 08A.
5. Staff copy/export the reviewed reply for their existing communication process.
   Copying does not mean sent. Durable history and approved-answer reuse are target
   deliverables; today's browser-only edits and checkbox do not satisfy them.

Included target scope: one organization, an agreed staff allowance and document
library, sourced drafts, durable review/history, approved-answer reuse with stale
source checks, usage boundaries and managed operating/support procedures.

Excluded: autonomous sending, autonomous CRM changes, public website chat,
unlimited usage/support, multiple connectors, general CRM replacement, and
arbitrary template-perfect questionnaire processing. One email connector and
bounded questionnaires remain conditional on buyer need and their engineering gates.
